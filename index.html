Bridging football and science

Doctor of Theoretical Physics with a football background, applying scientific methodology, custom-built analytical tools, and detailed video analysis to identify the factors that influence performance and results.
This approach has already proven its effectiveness through collaboration with elite clubs and professional footballers.

Data Analysis

Transforming match data into meaningful performance indicators. The focus is on understanding key metrics and parameters to reveal strengths, weaknesses, patterns, and opportunities for improvement. Every action performed by a player or team is analyzed, with particular attention given to the subtle details that can influence performance and ultimately change the outcome of a game.

Video Analysis

The findings from the data analysis are brought to life through video. Key moments are isolated and presented in focused sequences that reveal tactical patterns, technical details, and decision-making processes that often go unnoticed during a match.
